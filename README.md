# 🚀 SkillSync – Industry-Aligned Skill Intelligence Platform

**Bridging the Gap Between Academic Education and Industry Requirements**

SkillSync is an intelligent skill and career intelligence platform designed to bridge the employability gap between academic education and industry expectations. It helps students understand changing job market demands, identify skill gaps, explore career opportunities, and build industry-aligned learning roadmaps.

The platform brings labour market insights, career intelligence, training capacity analysis, and future skill predictions into one unified ecosystem.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many engineering graduates struggle to secure employment despite holding academic degrees because their skills and practical knowledge do not always align with current industry requirements.

SkillSync addresses this challenge by connecting academic skills, industry demands, and career development through data-driven intelligence.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Labour Market Intelligence
- Analyse industry hiring trends.
- Identify in-demand technical and professional skills.
- Understand changing employment requirements.
- Explore regional labour market insights.

### 2. Skill Gap & Career Intelligence
- Identify gaps between existing and required skills.
- Analyse career readiness.
- Provide personalized career recommendations.
- Suggest industry-aligned learning pathways.

### 3. Training Capacity Intelligence
- Evaluate available training opportunities.
- Analyse skill development resources.
- Identify training requirements and opportunities.

### 4. Future Skill Intelligence
- Identify emerging technologies and skills.
- Analyse future industry requirements.
- Support career planning based on evolving market demands.

### 5. Continuous Feedback Ecosystem
- Collect feedback from students and stakeholders.
- Improve skill recommendations.
- Support continuous platform improvement.

## 🔄 Platform Workflow

**Dashboard → Skill Gap Analysis → Career Roadmap → Industry Jobs**

1. Dashboard: Explore skill and labour market insights.
2. Skill Gap: Understand the difference between existing and required skills.
3. Roadmap: Discover recommended learning and development paths.
4. Industry Jobs: Explore relevant employment opportunities.

## 🌍 Regional Focus

SkillSync aims to provide regional employment intelligence, with a particular focus on Rajasthan.

The platform includes a focus on Alwar to explore regional skill requirements, employment opportunities, and industry-aligned career development.

## 💡 Our Vision

To create a smarter connection between education and employment by helping students become industry-ready through skill intelligence, career guidance, and data-driven insights.
